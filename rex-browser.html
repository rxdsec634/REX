Rex Browser · v0.1.0

The agent, in a browser.

An AI browser for Windows, Linux and Android with the REX agent in a side panel. It reads pages, clicks, types, fills forms and calls APIs to automate real work — while you watch and can take over at any moment. Browsing and search are free; the agent uses your rxdsec account.

Download Rex Browser Releases

The agent, built in

Web, browser and HTTP tools with 40+ model providers and adjustable thinking effort. Pause and take over mid-task — it never acts behind your back.

Dangerous-site protection

Known malware and phishing sites are blocked with a warning. Lists refresh every few hours and are checked on your device — the sites you visit are never sent anywhere.

HTTPS by default

Every http:// address is tried over HTTPS first; sites without it fall back and are marked “Not secure.” Bad certificates are never bypassed.

Private and fast

Ad and tracker blocking (Shields), Global Privacy Control, and a private mode the agent can’t see. Your data stays on your device.

Verified auto-updates

Updates come straight from GitHub releases and are checked against a SHA-256 checksum before they install. A mismatch is discarded.

One account, every device

Windows, Linux and Android, native on each, with the same rxdsec account — the one you already use for REX.
