What happens after the trial?＋

There is no automatic paid renewal. Your account shows the access available to you. Contact rxdsec about continuing access while paid plans are being finalised.

Are model costs included?＋

No. You connect your own cloud provider or local runtime. Any model-provider charges follow that provider’s pricing.

Does creating an account guarantee access?＋

Access can require approval. The account page shows whether your profile is approved, your plan, and your entitlement status.

Can I talk about a team deployment?＋

Yes. Use the contact links to discuss your requirements. Team features and commercial terms are provisional until agreed.
